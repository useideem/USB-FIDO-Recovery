Register with your conference badge

Step 1: Scan your badge

Register opens this computer's camera. Hold the QR code on your badge up to it; the scan stops on its own once the code is read.

Step 2: Enter your details

Fill in the details and press Register to add them.

The phone number is stored as digits only; it is what the demo uses as the User ID.

Step 3: Enroll iShield Key

Insert the attendee's iShield key, press the button, then touch the key when the browser asks. The key must have no PIN set: a PIN-less key answers with a touch on any phone, while a key with a PIN asks every one of them for it.

Desktop Chrome makes the key set a PIN before it will store a passkey, and every device that reads the key afterwards will demand that PIN. Enroll in Safari instead.

Step 4: Scan with your phone

The demo opens with your phone number as the User ID. Press Login there and touch your iShield key when the phone asks.
