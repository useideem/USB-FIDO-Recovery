Identify

Insert your iShield key, press Identify, then touch the key when the browser asks. Your User ID is read off the key and the partner page opens with it.

Desktop Chrome refuses to read a PIN-less key without a credential list, so Identify will not work here. Use a phone, or Safari on a Mac.
