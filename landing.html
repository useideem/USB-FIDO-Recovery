The Games

Your USB FIDO Key is your ticket to the games. Three steps and you are in.

  1. Pick up your key. Stop by the Registration Table in the expo hall and collect your USB FIDO Key. It is yours to take home.
  2. Get your phone ready. Enroll your device with the USB Key: cryptographic device binding, your mobile number validated directly with your carrier through Silent Network Authentication (SNA), and a mobile Passkey.
  3. Come play. Bring your USB Key to the closing party, Death to Passwords. Use it to play our sponsors' games, earn points and pick up treats.

Your points land on the Leader Board, which lights up when the party does.
